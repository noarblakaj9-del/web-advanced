Help & Support

How do I place an order?

Shopping in the Barça Official Store is very simple. Follow these steps:

  • Find the item you want
  • Add it to your basket
  • Check your cart and edit if needed
  • Fill in your details
  • Choose delivery method
  • Complete your purchase

Returns Policy

Some products cannot be returned such as personalised items, hygiene products, and used goods.

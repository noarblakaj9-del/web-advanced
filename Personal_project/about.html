Shopping in the Barça Official Store Spotify Camp Nou is very simple. Follow these steps to place your first order with us. Find the item you want to buy. In the product details, click on the add to my basket button. Check the items you have added to the basket. You can edit the basket as many times as you like (e.g. adding or removing products, customising them or changing the sizes). Fill in your details. Choose the delivery method. Complete your purchase as a guest or register with the Barça Official Store Spotify Camp Nou. You will then receive an order confirmation email. If you have made a purchase as a guest, remember that you can also create your Barça Official Store Spotify Camp Nou account to enjoy more benefits when you buy your items.
